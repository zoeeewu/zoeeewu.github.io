This project explores the relationship between lower-extremity muscle morphology and anthropometric characteristics, with the goal of predicting muscle maximum cross-sectional area (MaxCSA) using readily available individual measurements.

My work involved quantitative analysis of CT-derived muscle cross-sectional area and the development and evaluation of predictive models across thigh muscle groups. The findings highlight the potential of anthropometric measurements to estimate muscle size without relying on costly imaging procedures, while also revealing differences in predictive performance across muscle groups. The research analysis has been completed, and the manuscript is currently being prepared for submission.
