project_summary = """
This project investigates how botulinum neurotoxin (BoNT) treatment may influence ankle mechanical impedance and neuromuscular control during post-stroke walking. By applying controlled mechanical perturbations during gait, the study aims to characterize ankle responses and better understand changes in joint mechanical properties associated with BoNT treatment.

My work involves human-subject experiments, Vicon motion-capture data processing, and biomechanical data analysis. I have also been involved in integrating gait datasets and applying machine learning methods to predict ankle and shank motion and joint torque in individuals with and without stroke. Beyond gaining experience across the research workflow, from data collection to analysis, I have learned to troubleshoot unexpected challenges by adapting data-processing scripts, refining Vicon workflows, and updating skeletal templates to improve motion-capture processing.
